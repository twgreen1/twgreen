Tuna Pasta Bake

A creamy, cheesy tuna and bacon pasta bake packed with broccoli, peas and spinach.

Serves: 4 Prep: 15 mins Cook: 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta
  • 2 tins tuna, drained
  • 3 rashers bacon, sliced
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut into small florets
  • 100–150g peas
  • 200–250g spinach
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1–2 tsp garlic paste
  • 40g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 100g grated cheddar, plus extra for the top
  • 1 tsp Dijon or English mustard
  • ½ tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Black pepper
  • Salt, to taste
  • Optional: breadcrumbs or grated Parmesan for the topping

Method

  1. Preheat the oven Preheat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / Gas Mark 6.
  2. Cook the bacon Slice the bacon into small pieces and fry in a large pan over medium-high heat until browned and slightly crispy. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  3. Cook the pasta and broccoli Cook the pasta in salted water, taking it off 2 minutes before the packet's recommended cooking time. Add the broccoli for the last 3–4 minutes of cooking. Drain everything together.
  4. Make the sauce Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Add the garlic paste and cook for about 30 seconds. Stir in the flour and cook for about 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the milk until smooth, then keep stirring until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the cheese and vegetables Take the sauce off the heat and stir in 100g cheddar, the mustard and mixed herbs. Add plenty of black pepper and only a little salt, as the bacon and cheese are already salty. Stir in the peas and spinach, allowing the spinach to wilt down into the sauce.
  6. Combine Put the pasta and broccoli into a large baking dish. Flake in the drained tuna and add the cooked bacon. Pour over the cheese sauce and mix everything together well.
  7. Top it Sprinkle generously with grated cheddar. Add breadcrumbs or Parmesan if using.
  8. Bake Bake for 20–25 minutes, until bubbling and golden on top.

Optional Extras

Make it your own: Add chilli flakes for some heat, a squeeze of lemon juice for freshness, or breadcrumbs mixed with the cheese for an extra-crispy topping.
