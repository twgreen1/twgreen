Butter Chicken

Creamy butter chicken with korma and tandoori spices, tomato purée, spinach and rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 mug rice
  • Butter (obvs)
  • A couple of handfuls of spinach
  • 500g chicken
  • 150ml crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • ½ jar korma spice paste
  • 2 tsp tandoori spice blend
  • 2 chicken stock cubes
  • Olive oil
  • 2 mugs water

Method

  1. Prepare the chicken and spinachSlice the chicken and wash the spinach.
  2. Toast the riceMelt a generous amount of butter in a pan. Stir in the rice until it starts to become nicely toasty.
  3. Cook the riceAdd twice as much water as rice — 2 mugs of water for 1 mug of rice. Put the lid on. When it starts to boil, turn the heat down so it stays at a simmer and set a 10-minute rice timer.
  4. DO NOT TAKE THE LID OFFSeriously. Leave the rice alone while it cooks.
  5. Make the spice baseHeat some olive oil in another pan. Add the korma spice paste, tandoori spice blend and tomato purée. Stir everything together in the oil.
  6. Cook the chickenWhen it starts smelling fragrant, add the chicken and stir regularly while it cooks.
  7. Rest the riceWhen the first 10-minute rice timer goes off, turn the heat off and move the rice away from the hot ring. Set another 10-minute timer and leave the lid on.
  8. Finish the curryKeep stirring the chicken. When the chicken is nearly done, crumble in the 2 chicken stock cubes and add the crème fraîche. Stir it like it's hot.
  9. Finish cookingKeep cooking and stirring until the chicken is fully cooked and the second rice timer goes off.
  10. Add the spinachStir the spinach into the curry and cook until wilted.
  11. ServeFluff up the rice, pile the butter chicken on top, and enjoy.
Note: Make sure the chicken is fully cooked before serving. And keep it off the walls and in the pan!
