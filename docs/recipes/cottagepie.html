Cottage Pie

Classic beef cottage pie with a rich, thick gravy and crispy mashed potato topping.

Serves 4 Prep 20 min Cook 1 hr

Ingredients

  • 150g pancetta, diced
  • 500g beef mince
  • 500g frozen sofritto mix (onion, carrot, celery)
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 beef stock cubes
  • 350ml water (for stock)
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 5 medium potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 100ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper, plus more to taste
  • 150g frozen petits pois

Method

  1. Fry the pancettaFry the pancetta in a dry pan over medium heat for a few minutes until golden and crisp, releasing its fat.
  2. Brown the minceAdd the beef mince to the pan with the pancetta and fry over medium-high heat until well browned, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain off any excess fat if needed.
  3. Add veg and garlicAdd the frozen sofritto mix and garlic to the pan. Fry until the vegetables have thawed and any excess liquid has mostly cooked off, and the mixture starts to catch a little colour.
  4. Fry the puréeStir in the tomato purée and fry for 1 minute to deepen the flavour before adding any liquid.
  5. Add flourSprinkle in the flour and stir well to coat the meat and veg evenly, cooking for a minute to remove the raw flour taste.
  6. Build the sauceCrumble in the beef stock cubes and pour in the water. Add the Worcestershire sauce and bay leaves. Stir well to combine, making sure the flour doesn't clump.
  7. SimmerBring to a gentle simmer and cook for about 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and rich.
  8. Add the peasStir in the frozen petits pois and simmer for a further 3–5 minutes, just until heated through, so they stay bright and don't turn mushy.
  9. Boil the potatoesMeanwhile, boil the potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 20 minutes.
  10. MashDrain the potatoes and mash with the milk, butter, salt and pepper until smooth. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  11. AssemblePreheat the oven to gas mark 7 (220°C/200°C fan). Remove the bay leaves from the mince, then transfer the mince to an oven dish. Spread the mash evenly over the top, then drag a fork over the surface to create ridges for crisping.
  12. BakeBake for about 20 minutes, until the top is golden and crisp, and the filling is bubbling at the edges.
Note: Remove the bay leaves before topping with mash — easy to forget! For extra browning on top, finish under the grill for 2–3 minutes after baking.
