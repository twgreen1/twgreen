The Official Rules & Regulations

1. Volume of a Cup of Tea

The cup from which the tea is drunk is to contain a minimum of 250ml of liquid tea
In the event where the mug is larger (including, but not limited to, sports mugs or those whose capacity is 350ml or greater), only one cup of tea is to be counted.
Small cups and mugs, including but not limited to, espresso cups, Asian tasting cups, or half-filled mugs, are not to be counted as a cup of tea.

2. Composition of a Cup of Tea

The beverage is to be brewed from the Camellia sinensis plant (including, but not limited to, English Breakfast, Earl Grey, Green, Oolong, and White tea).
The tea may include additives, including but not limited to milk, sugar, honey, and lemon, which have no effects on the validity of the cup of tea, subject to the other conditions being met.

3. Validity of a Cup of Tea

The cup of tea is only to be counted if it is fully consumed.
No more than a reasonable swallow of liquid is to remain at the bottom of the mug.
A void cup is one which is left to go entirely stone-cold and abandoned and is therefore not to be added to the tea counter.
The cup of tea is to be prepared or ordered by the competitor for themselves, or to be accepted as part of a genuinely offered round.
One competitor may not force the other to consume a cup of tea under duress.
The cup of tea is only to be counted if it is consumed within the county of Nottinghamshire.
Special Circumstances:

Should either competitor be kidnapped by Somali pirates and forced under duress to consume a cup of tea by their captors, only half a cup of tea is to be counted as a show of their commitment to the competition.
Competitor Conduct:

The competitor must be deemed to be of sound mind and not under the influence of alcohol or illicit substances at the time of tea consumption, unless prescribed by a qualified doctor with a PhD and an MBE as awarded by the late Queen Elizabeth II. May she Rest in Peace.
If walking within the grounds of Nottingham Trent University, any such competitor is to be deducted 10 cups of tea from the counter as punishment for being an enemy of the State.

4. Appeals and Winners

A cup of tea is to be logged on the counter within one hour of the completion of consumption. This counter is to be found at https://www.twgreen.uk/tea.
If a competitor disputes the accuracy or validity of the running total:
An independent body of arbitration shall be established to investigate possible corruptions or infractions which may or may not have occurred.
The jury is to be composed of the residents of the competitors’ house, past and present, excluding any active competitors. This jury is to give a unanimous ruling in order for any rulings to be valid.
The competition is deemed to have ended upon termination of the competitors’ rental contract at their house.
The winner of the competition is the competitor who ends the competition with the most cups of tea logged on the tea counter.
In the event of a tie:
There is to be a race between the competitors in which they are to consume 300ml of tea.
The first person to finish the cup of tea is to be determined the winner.
This race is to be umpired by a live audience composed of Mr Akhtar and himself.

5. Prizes

The winner of the competition is to receive a prize.
This prize is to be any drink of their choice from the Roebuck Inn, Nottingham, to be paid for by loser.
The loser of the competition is to receive a prize.
This prize is to be a pint of Lilley’s cider, which is to be downed.
